# Krag Kings — persistent project context

Updated 6 October 2026. This file records explicit user direction for future work.

## Confirmed direction

| Decision | User direction |
| --- | --- |
| Narrative status | The narrative material is a fun way to understand the world and lore. It is **not the final scenario**. This applies especially to design files 09 and 13 and story-derived examples elsewhere. |
| Platform | Downloadable Windows build; prioritize visual quality. The earlier browser-first proposal is superseded for this POC. |
| POC | One playable tactical mission at full presentation quality, squad customization, and a compact persistent injury/bionic recovery loop. |
| Wider-game presentation | Concept art for the campaign **UX/UI**, base building, and world map, in the same style as the existing concept art. Campaign art must show the playable interface and decisions, not cinematic key art. These additions are visual presentation scope; they do not expand the playable POC into the full campaign or base-building game. |
| Art fidelity | High-quality, detailed 3D that faithfully matches the selected concept art. Character quality is a major production priority, not an optional final polish pass. |
| Hero-character priority | The user reaffirmed that Krags and Nibs are the game's core icons. Battlefields have limited characters and combat is turn-based; prioritize close-up character fidelity and high-quality animation over premature mesh reduction. Continue reference comparisons and refinement passes for natural anatomy first, then bionic add-ons. “AAA” and “wow graphics” are quality targets, not a claim that the current assets have passed review. Actual unit-count and frame-time budgets still require measurement. |
| Facial performance and deformation | Very expressive facial animation is required for actions, close-ups and future cutscenes, with anatomically credible facial skeletons and body rigs that support muscle deformation. This is an implementation requirement, not a claim that the initial body rigs meet it. Facial bones, expression/pose corrective shapes, eye/lid behavior and muscle/joint deformation need actual source and engine verification. The user authorized concept-consistent provisional mouth interiors (teeth, gums, tongue) and expression sheets for review; these remain provisional until reviewed. |
| Species locomotion | Krags walk with convincing weight and ground impact. Nibs are light-footed, stealthy and nimble. Express the distinction through animation, foot contact, body weight transfer and appropriate sound/effects; validate it on dune slopes. |
| Latest visual review — realism and motion | The user rejects the current cartoony appearance and unnatural arm orientation, especially during locomotion. Both species are humanoids: require smooth, natural human-like joint motion while preserving the species' weight/personality. Krag muscles must read as coherent anatomy rather than rounded separate masses. A normal-looking mouth opening and a metal jaw that does not clip retained anatomy are required. These are current defects to correct, not accepted stylization. |
| Whole-body movement and idle | The torso must participate naturally in locomotion. Coordinate pelvis, spine, shoulders, arms and head with planted feet and weight transfer, preserving Krag heaviness and Nib agility. Stationary characters need a visible but restrained idle with breathing, subtle weight shifts and attentive acting rather than a rigid resting pose. |
| Nib ear acting | Nib ears must be able to twitch. Author subtle independent ear motion, rooted and deforming naturally at the ear bases, alongside facial/action animation; preserve the selected ear silhouette. |
| Action expressions and inspection | Facial expressions must accompany every animation, not only a separate expression demonstration. Each unit acts independently: selecting and triggering the other unit while the first finishes must work. Pan, orbit and zoom remain available during actions so full motion and close-ups can be inspected. |
| Nib fur and cinematic masters | Follow the concept: visible mottled skin with fine facial/body fuzz, fuller ear/head fur. Do not redesign Nibs as fully fur-covered. The runtime representation must remain realistic and responsive on the user's RTX 2060 reference machine; avoid costly full fur simulation and measure actual frame time/VRAM. Also retain detailed editable Blender masters for a realistically furry Nib and muscular Krag for animation/cinematics. Runtime and cinematic detail must preserve the same character identity and coverage. |
| Visual/performance balance | The user explicitly prioritizes satisfying visuals and fluid framerate together, authorizing sensible sacrifices in each engine to achieve the best feasible requirement coverage. Tune from actual results on the reference rig; preserve identity, faces, silhouette and readable movement while reducing costly details/effects where justified. Maximum settings or exact parity of renderer features are not acceptance goals in themselves. Record the tradeoffs and actual internal/output resolution. |
| Final demo evidence | Deliver screenshots and a demo video from each running engine, showing rendering, dune movement, camera inspection, animations/facial acting and the different models. Record separately from performance sampling; Blender renders supplement, but do not replace, actual Unity/Unreal evidence. |
| Nib tongue color | Nibs have dark blue tongues. This is an explicit character-design requirement for mouth models, materials and facial-expression review. |
| Personality and acting | Nibs are playful engineers: physically weak, a little cowardly, light-footed and nimble. They may cheekily stick out their dark blue tongues while retaining the capable adult identity of the concepts. Krags are serious brute force: they love big guns and smashing skulls, are fearless and feel little pain; they do not clown around like Nibs. Carry the contrast through facial/body animation, close-ups, cutscenes, voices and quips. Nib caution/flinching is acting direction; automatic panic, routing or loss of player control is not approved by this personality decision. |
| Customization | Authored clothing and armor pieces; authored tattoo designs; clothing/tattoo color and tattoo placement controls; fixed body proportions. Equipped armor must be visible. |
| Clothing and equipment by species | Krags prefer armor and bionics, with minimal clothing because their hardy skin resists the desert. Their customization emphasizes armor and mechanical replacements, retaining recognizable anatomy. Nibs have fewer bionic choices and more practical desert-clothing/equipment variety. Minimal Krag clothing does not mean minimal armor. |
| Replacement anatomy | Forearms/hands, lower legs, eyes, jaws, **and complete arms and legs** must be represented in the playable customization/bionic system. Species compatibility still matters. Exact item catalogue and new full-limb designs require resolution. |
| Nib replacement limit | Nib implants restore ordinary anatomical function only; they are not upgrades and grant no additional strength, movement, aiming or tool capability. Their technical strengths come from personality, learned skills and separately carried or vehicle-supported equipment. Earlier source-pack enhancement/perk examples are superseded. Exact strain capacity remains a tuning proposal. |
| Merchandising | Character masters should support both collectible figures and printed/illustrated merchandise. Manufacturing variants and runtime models must derive from the same approved designs. |
| Engine decision | Compare Unreal and Unity before choosing. Unity has an initial idle measurement, but the final matched comparison and engine selection remain pending. |
| Mission objective | Depot salvage: release, load and extract an engine, with boarding opportunities. This is a selected gameplay objective independent of the narrated story. |
| Strategic concepts | Explore the desert scrapyard world, travel between strategic locations, and a fort growing from tents/wreckage into an industrial stronghold. Story, names and geography stay provisional. |
| World-map scale | A much broader, sparser regional view, with small settlements separated by extensive terrain. Use the strategic camera distance and spacing of a Total War map as a reference while preserving Krag Kings' own visual identity. This does not approve another game's campaign mechanics. |
| Tin Can identity and scope | Tall, round, refitted scrap tin-can/barrel machinery with extensive modular customization and varied options. Only Nibs may pilot it; the machine cannot board vehicles. Concept art and integration planning only for this POC; no playable Tin Can. |
| Concept review | The user approved the modular barrel-shaped Tin Can concept direction (sheet 14: “tin can is great”). The cinematic campaign sheet 11 was rejected for the campaign UX/UI brief; the compact world map 13 needs a wider, sparser replacement. Base-building art and revised campaign/map concepts still require review. Tin Can approval does not settle the production fit/manufacturing details. |
| Audio | Music, sound effects and English character speech with distinct quips/personalities. Krags sound deep and gruff; Nibs are higher-pitched and speak faster while remaining adult and intelligible. Use AI-generated voices for now, with the option to replace them with actors if finances allow. No particular accent or voice-generation provider has been selected. |
| Performance reference | The user's ASUS ROG Strix G17 is the chosen medium-range reference machine. Local Windows/CIM and NVIDIA queries verified model G712LV, Intel Core i7-10750H, NVIDIA RTX 2060 with 6144 MiB VRAM, about 32 GB RAM (31.8 GiB reported), and active 1920 × 1080 display. These are inventory results, not a game benchmark. |
| Budget and deadline | No limit or deadline set yet. Plan toward the best POC; the user will handle external resourcing. This is not an agreed delivery date or authorization to incur purchases. |
| Ambiguity | Ask the user about unclear or material unresolved choices instead of silently guessing. |
| Engine-demo implementation | User has now authorized implementation, multiple subagents and Blender/tool use for matched Unity and Unreal Windows demos. Each must render a detailed concept-faithful Krag and Nib on sandy dunes; left-click selects, right-click runs to terrain position with elevation respected. Keys demonstrate melee, shooting and hit animations. Multiple bionic variants are required, including the Krag crusher claw; compatible exaggerated Krag designs may be invented, while Nib implants restore function without upgrades. |
| Reliability | User wants top-quality, bug-free work. Translate that into explicit acceptance evidence and no known release-blocking defects; do not promise proof of absence of every defect. |

## Authority and status

Explicit current user decisions take precedence over older draft proposals. Approved model sheets will resolve perspective inconsistencies and define missing surfaces while preserving concept likeness. Existing written handedness and fit rules remain useful constraints, but a major conflict with the concepts must be surfaced for resolution.

The repository initially contained 14 Markdown design documents and 10 PNG concept sheets. No playable implementation, meshes, rigs, textures, animations, or measured performance results were present. `game-design-notes.md`, cited by the overview as an earlier source, was not found in this workspace. Claims in the pack about earlier conversations are documented source claims, not independently verified history.

The original documents remain the design source pack. New planning documents distinguish confirmed requirements, proposed production details, pending decisions, and future scope. Numerical combat values remain tuning proposals unless explicitly approved.

## Current planning work

The supplied documents and concept art have been reviewed and the integration/production plan is recorded. Implementation is underway in `benchmark/`. Both engines now have Windows executables and actual runtime images; neither character has passed artistic acceptance and no engine has been selected. See the benchmark's [current evidence and status](benchmark/README.md).

Unity's current Windows build `c394a9ba909b477fb315daa0072f83c7` passes 17 functional checks across seven variants, independent overlapping actions, facial/body corrective activation, terrain movement and actual Krag firing direction. Its shared content fingerprint remains unchanged. The [actual action-view review](benchmark/evidence/unity/20261006-action-bust-review/README.md) now keeps the raised Krag weapon visible and shows restoration-only Nib HUD text. Character likeness and deformation still fail artistic review. The current native input suite is incomplete: preserved attempts distinguish an offscreen test destination, startup timing, and lost Windows foreground. No Unity quick-Shift bug has been established. Earlier native-1080p samples averaged 40.77 FPS Full moving and 57.95 FPS Balanced moving; those predate the current assets/build and are not locked-60 or final comparison claims. The [previous actual Unity recording](benchmark/evidence/unity/20261006-sand-v2-showcase/README.md) passes capture checks and its audio-duration gate (71.978667 seconds for a 72-second sequence). It belongs to build `b5fa6342a69f462d8e2f6510ee0056e6`; its character likeness and old Krag action framing remain failed.

Unreal's native code, seven isolated character imports and revised Windows package pass technical checks. It now uses the same shared asset fingerprint as Unity's current build. Its [controlled native-1080p samples](benchmark/unreal/evidence/performance-sand-v2/summary.json) averaged 47.36 FPS Full moving and 52.96 FPS Balanced moving; idle samples averaged 50.11 and 55.54 FPS respectively. Balanced lowers software Lumen GI/reflections to High while other recorded groups remain Epic. These short samples have variable laptop clocks, unaccepted skin/art and no sustained-60 or engine-winner claim. The [first actual Unreal video](benchmark/unreal/evidence/showcase-sand-v2/review.json) records real movement, variants and expressions, but is rejected as a synchronized demo: its 72-second sequence exported only 34.197 seconds of engine audio because silent submix blocks appear to be omitted. A stricter duration gate rejects that WAV. The [new actual Profile recording](benchmark/unreal/evidence/showcase-profile-audio-fixed/README.md) now contains exactly 72.000 seconds of engine audio, without padding or time stretching. Its 17 decoded frames show improved Krag action framing and pass capture-integrity checks. Profile skin remains a candidate, character likeness still fails, and listening/fine synchronization remain pending. Those earlier performance samples belong to the previous Generic-skin package, not this new recording package.

The user confirmed dismissing the Windows picker, and a [read-only recheck](benchmark/unreal/evidence/picker-overlay-recheck-20261006.json) verifies it is gone. Unreal's [actual packaged orbit correction](benchmark/unreal/evidence/native-input-orbit-fixed/) then passes camera interaction during actions. A later [native modifier test](benchmark/unreal/evidence/native-input-modifier-failure/) proves a real defect: Shift released in the same input frame as a right-click can make the accepted move run instead of walk. A press-time cursor/modifier fix is prepared; its native compile/package and full interaction proof are pending. The complete native suite has not passed.

Character art remains an active failure gate. The separate Krag v9h source has five actual views: broad profile and lower-gum tusk attachment improve, and upper-lip/nose Jaw influence is zero. Smooth facial planes, rectangular mouth opening, obscured dental anatomy, eyelid folds and the rigid scarf still fail. A cloth-only simulation and true-commissure/contact repair are prepared. Nib v5f's actual Jaw repair removes the stretched mouth curtain and exposes its dark-blue tongue. Separate v5g facial planes retain the closed neck but introduce a hard Blink patch at a binary nasal-support boundary; the strict neutral oral-visibility gate still fails. A saved-source eyelid audit and bounded continuous repair are in progress. Neither character study is promoted to the engines. The [separate equipped weapon study](benchmark/art/weapons/study-v2/equipped-review.json) preserves prop geometry after an observed import-transform repair and has two inspected firing views; surface wear and complete contact review remain pending. Further anatomy, clothing, facial/mouth, fur, bionic and cinematic-master work remains. Technical import success does not establish concept likeness.
