# Krag Kings project instructions

Read [PROJECT_CONTEXT.md](PROJECT_CONTEXT.md) before making design, art, scope, or implementation decisions.

- Narrative playthroughs are world/lore illustrations, **not the final scenario, campaign script, or mandatory sequence of events**. Do not promote their dialogue, outcomes, injuries, characters' roles, or mission order into requirements without a separate decision.
- Preserve the existing concept-art identity. The user requires high-quality 3D suitable for an investor demonstration and consistent merchandise. Unresolved views, new designs, and significant likeness changes need questions and review, not invented canon.
- Consult the current realization plan and decision register linked from the project context. The original browser-first/placeholder POC proposal does not define the new delivery target.
- Ask about material design ambiguities; identify proposals and tuning values explicitly. Continue independent work while answers are pending.
- Preserve source documents and selected concept images. Do not claim a build, model, benchmark, or validation exists until it does.
