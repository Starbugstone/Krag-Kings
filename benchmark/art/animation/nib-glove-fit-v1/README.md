# Nib fitted glove v1 — actual source and poses

Replaced four old floating palmar rivets and the box wrist strap with a dorsal leather reinforcement, a fitted wrist band and four supported brass rivets. The new parts follow the actual hand triangles and inherit their barycentric skin weights. Bind and animation are unchanged; the old parts are archived in the source.

The saved source and 18 posed palm/back/edge renders exist. Inspected Idle dorsal/palmar and Run edge views show the rivets seated on the panel and a wrist band following the hand. This is a clay shape check; it does not establish final leather/metal appearance or complete collision clearance over every action.

The forearm still has disconnected tubular wraps, the free hand needs action-specific grip poses, and the right hand remains unchanged. Clothing, face, groom, final likeness and both engine imports remain open. See `glove-fit.json`, frozen recipes and memory records for exact execution evidence.
