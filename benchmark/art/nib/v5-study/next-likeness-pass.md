# Next likeness pass: dominant forms before detail

Current v4b is structurally portable but visually unaccepted. Its procedural face looks flat, the iris discs sit outside the lids, hair coverage is sparse and the garment construction is weak. Runtime reduction is a separate operation to make engine testing feasible; it does not fix these art defects.

## Facial construction

Use the CC0 animation head only as a topology foundation. Its existing orbital, nostril and lip loops provide continuous surfaces that v4b lacks. Adapt the compact feline muzzle, short triangular nose, pointed adult cheek planes and restrained lower lip against sheet 02. The present clay trial still looks too human and retains source human-ear relief; both are explicit failed checks.

- Preserve original UV topology and source provenance when appending the native asset.
- Identify the original ear face sets, remove the two human ears and rebuild the lateral skull. Exactly two large fennec ears remain.
- Preserve matched source eye/lid geometry and warp the orbit as a region. Fit a convex eyeball behind it; iris and pupil belong on its front surface rather than floating discs. Check neutral, gaze extremes and full blink in front and three-quarter views.
- Form upper/lower lips, philtrum and nose continuously. Keep the mouth sealed in neutral; adapt the approved provisional dark-blue tongue/interior behind it. Check Smile, Frown, JawOpen and TongueOut together for collisions.
- Retain the named FaceRoot controls and portable morph contract. Re-author deltas on the new topology; transferring the old malformed eyelid shapes blindly is inappropriate.

## Groom and ears

The current scalp guides frequently turn back into the skull, explaining why thousands of strands still read as short sparse stubble. Place roots against the actual adapted scalp and direct curves along its tangent with an outward clearance. Add layered cream forehead/temple/occipital tufts around the goggles, keeping eyes and central forehead readable. Ear fur needs a fuller fringe and interior clumps, with pink inner skin visible. Preserve the confirmed mottled-skin/fine-fuzz body coverage.

The native cinematic master should use the same guide field as runtime. Increase strands per clump and longitudinal detail with independent random streams so changing density does not move the guide layout or redesign the silhouette. It remains outstanding.

## Hands and cloth

The CC0 `Hand  - Realistic` has coherent palm, finger webs and knuckles. Adapt its proportions and joint positions into the current fixed Nib scale, then fit fingerless glove shells to the same underlying hand. Recheck gun grip and melee fist against the actual finger skeleton; nearest-bone weights alone do not prove contact.

The v4b generator reuses the left-hand transverse finger ordering on the right, placing the named right Index bone on the little-finger side relative to its thumb. Correct the right anatomical ordering in the next source rig, preserve stable bone names, and regenerate matching bind-carrying clips. The current performance derivative intentionally preserves the existing skeleton to isolate reduction, so it does not fix this grip defect.

Replace the broad scarf sheet with several compressed draped loops around the neck and shoulders, preserving neck clearance throughout Shoot/Hit. The bib needs body conformity, soft seam tension and pocket volume; seams should follow the fabric rather than decorating a rigid rectangle. Recheck back straps and crotch/leg folds in side/back views.

The next clothing pass must also recover the reference's tapered waist, broad gloved palms and fuller baggy cropped trousers with sewn side cargo pockets. The current repeated sinusoidal leg profile reads as inflated segments rather than gathered fabric. Author broad gravity/tension folds from waistband, crotch, knee and rolled hem, then smaller wrinkles; do not retain the periodic ring pattern as final cloth. Boots need a continuous shaped upper/toe/heel, layered stitched sole/welt, fitted leather straps and readable buckles. The current rounded boxes and ankle spheres are not a footwear-quality pass. These remain in scope after the face/groom correction.

## Skin pattern

The v4b skin generator's sinusoidal freckle mask produces uniform fine speckling. Sheet 02 instead has larger irregular tan/charcoal patches concentrated around cheeks, temples, shoulders and arms, with finer pores between them. Replace the periodic mask with multiscale irregular mottling and anatomical region masks; leave quieter muzzle/forehead regions. Keep pore/bump amplitude small enough that pores do not become regular pits. This comes after facial construction and does not alter the current runtime derivative's comparison materials.

Current overlapping per-component UV layouts are suitable for tiled materials but do not guarantee region-specific face/body pigment placement. The next texture pass must use fitted unique skin UV regions or explicit region masks baked into maps, then inspect the exact imported PBR result. A procedural Blender-only shader is insufficient.
