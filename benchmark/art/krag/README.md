# Krag source and review record

Status: editable authored review model; artistic acceptance and engine import validation pending. This is not an approved merchandise master or a claim of concept-equivalent quality.

The visual authority is the original [Krag sheet](../../../krag-kings-design/concept-art/01-krag-character-sheet.png) and [heavy bionics sheet](../../../krag-kings-design/concept-art/07-crusher-claw-and-metal-jaw-sheet.png). The model is original Blender geometry and authored motion, without third-party meshes or motion capture. Skin microstructure incorporates [Mud Cracked Dry 03](https://polyhaven.com/a/mud_cracked_dry_03), a CC0 scan processed by Dario Barresi and photographed by Dimitrios Savva. The downloaded originals, URLs and SHA256 hashes are preserved in `reference-materials/mud_cracked_dry_03/source.json`; diffuse is tinted, relief is restrained and the other material families remain procedural.

`Krag_Master.blend` contains the high-resolution unified anatomy, separate fitted clothing/armor, modular anatomical replacements, 66-bone weighted skeleton in generated v7, 25 facial/body corrective morphs, and seven authored motion actions. The current `../../tools/krag/build_krag.py` and its helpers include **pending v8 changes**, so running them does not reproduce the pinned v7 source exactly; use `-- --skip-render` to generate a new review revision without rendering. `AUTHORING_STATUS.json` separates generated evidence from code-only work. The source model faces Blender -Y, with Z up, at metre scale. The proposed regular-Krag crown height is 2.107 m. The ankle rest joints are at X ±0.19 m, Y 0.026 m, Z 0.24 m. These dimensions are benchmark proportions, not newly approved lore.

The natural variant is the first likeness review target. The crusher replaces the full anatomical left arm and adds a mechanical jaw; the separate iron-jaw/eye variant retains natural arms. The right piston-shin variant removes the matching boot and lower-trouser region. Crusher, iron jaw and reinforced piston mechanics are Krag-only; they must not be offered to Nibs.

Animations are authored in place at 30 fps: Idle 61 frames, Walk 37, Run 19, Melee 37, Shoot 31, Hit 28, FacePerformance 180. The rig includes Root, Pelvis, Spine, Chest, Neck, Head, Jaw; paired Clavicle/UpperArm/LowerArm/Hand and Thigh/Shin/Foot/Toe chains; and three claw controls. The full hand silhouette includes four modeled fingers and an opposed thumb, and v5 adds articulated finger/thumb controls. Movement speed, contact correction and action triggering belong to the engine harness.

Review renders are actual meshes. The first and second crusher perspectives exposed excessive eye visibility, separated abdominal volumes, weak finger silhouette, unsuitable footwear geometry and insufficient cloth construction. The third authoring pass addresses those defects and switches the likeness review to the natural variant. The natural front render exposed armor buried in the deltoid and unsuitable spherical steel toe caps; the fourth pass lifts the armor clear, drapes the scarf outside the torso, authors thin formed toe armor and adopts scanned fracture detail. Further comparison is required; these corrections do not constitute approval.

All subsequent heavy authoring, rendering and export tasks run serially through the project's `Run-HeavyTask.ps1` memory guard. Generation, individual view renders and export run as separate processes. The editable master retains source materials; the first explicit PBR bake has completed and its 56 portable maps are present. `../../tools/krag/export_krag.py` prepares portable OpenGL-normal PBR tiles and saves the runtime source (topology reduction is optional and disabled for shape-keyed meshes); a separate fresh invocation with `-- --export-only` exports standalone FBX variants and clips. Each runtime variant combines its selected modular assembly into one skinned mesh with shared material slots, including the right-hand cannon (visible in runtime, omitted from the neutral source likeness review). File existence and importer checks must be verified before claiming delivery.


## Versioned likeness review

All versions below remain **failed / not artistically accepted**. A buildable mesh is not proof of likeness or investor readiness.

- v3 natural front: anatomy and neutral scowl superseded the original crusher view. Armor intersected the deltoid; spherical toe caps and neck scarf were unsuitable.
- v5 actual natural front: `renders/Krag_Natural_Front_v5.png`; source SHA256 `e79dcaf50dd7bad94f4a5e62b877083a17054bbd59122d7380c52559f5071c1f`. The matching `.meta.json` preserves exact generation, source hash, review-script hash, frame and camera. Source has 46 bones and 1,577,028 triangles across mutually exclusive modules.
- v5 improved head scale, continuous shoulder skin, clearance of the pauldron and formed toe shells. It still fails against the approved reference: scarf resembles a rigid neck brace; trousers have regular parallel ribbing and cone-shaped thighs; arm silhouettes read as muscle balls; skin is pale/chalky with oversized, uniform cracks; pauldron lacks layered curved construction; fingers remain blunt and pawlike; facial anatomy and close-up expressive deformation are insufficient.
- v6 has now been generated and rendered, and remains **failed / unaccepted**. It adds warm/tighter skin microstructure with separate facial skin, a deep U-draped scarf, fewer broad irregular cloth pressure folds, layered curved shoulder plates, smoother arm/wrist transitions, provisional oral cavity, hybrid facial control bones and 25 soft-tissue/body-pose morph targets. It also replaces the Run cycle and adds Walk using analytic planted foot targets. Its actual front and head renders show improvements in warmth and hand orientation, but close-up quality is still inadequate.

`krag_face.py` defines the FaceRoot subtree, skeletal eyes/jaw/tongue, expression controls and the coordinate-invariant portable driver manifest. `FacePerformance` is a serious acting test (focus, suspicion, contained satisfaction, irritation and intimidation), with no clowning or playful tongue extension. Teeth, gums, tongue and cavity designs are provisional anatomy authorized for review. Closed-mouth neutral identity remains the baseline.

`krag_locomotion.py` declares Walk 1.15 m/s, 1.2 s, stance 0.62; Run 3.2 m/s, 0.6 s, stance 0.42. Left contact is normalized 0, right contact 0.5. Source clips include their closing frame. Mathematical ankle targets and skin-contact evidence are separate: exact solver error will be recorded, and rendered sole poses plus runtime dune contact still require inspection.

Heavy v5 generation: peak private memory 1,034 MB, minimum available memory 11,546 MB, peak commit 53%. Fresh v5 front render: 1,662 MB / 11,115 MB / 56%, exit 0. Heavy jobs remain serialized with two Blender threads through `Run-HeavyTask.ps1`; they do not imply the earlier host crash was caused by Blender.

Captures: `renders/Krag_Natural_Front_v6.png`, `renders/Krag_Natural_Head_v6.png`, each with a matching hash sidecar. Mathematical ankle checks cover 112 samples, with maximum actual-bone/target error 4.71e-8 m; mesh soles and runtime grounding remain unverified.

v6 failed close-up checks: brow forms look like detached horns; eyes look like flat lenses under bars; under-eye cavities are excessively hollow; some forehead wrinkle tubes float; scarf still resembles a neck brace; trouser hip union has a top ledge and skin protrudes below the belt. Natural hero likeness remains the priority over further bionic decoration.

v7 has now been generated and rendered, and remains **failed / unaccepted**. It adds a connected frontal-bone volume, fills the under-eye cheek plane, recesses actual eyeball spheres inside Boolean orbital cavities, reshapes curved eyelid rims, and replaces oversized flat irises with smaller detailed amber optics. It removes floating crease tubes, tapers the trouser hip transition and removes hidden under-trouser skin that protruded. A localized physical cloth simulation settles the scarf against temporary neck/torso collision surfaces; the actual simulation completed, but review shows it still reads as a thin bib rather than the reference’s thick looped layers.

First portable exports will be marked **review candidate / unaccepted** so engine imports can expose shader, morph and animation defects while art iteration continues. They are not approval to call the model investor-ready.


Required scope still outstanding: the current mechanical arm is a complete arm, while `Krag_Piston` replaces only the lower leg. A complete leg requires an authored hip/thigh mechanism and a clean split in the joined trouser mesh. This remains required after the first natural-hero engine import. Facial expression deformation, firearm grip/aim, locomotion sole contact, material fidelity after baking and all bionic replacement seams must be checked on actual exported meshes.


The v7 source also authors facial control curves in every body clip: watchful neutral/blink in Idle, focused walking, controlled exertion in Run, forceful scowl and contained satisfaction in Melee, sighting focus in Shoot, and low-pain irritation in Hit. `FacePerformance` remains a separate acting test. These tracks must be verified in exported clips; existence of authored code is not proof of final facial quality. The muscular editable Blender master is preserved separately from portable runtime assemblies for animation and cinematic work.

Actual captures are `renders/Krag_Natural_Front_v7.png` and `renders/Krag_Natural_Head_v7.png`, with matching metadata sidecars. FBX exports and roundtrip verification are separate stages and are not implied by successful texture baking.


## First portable candidate: v7

Four assembled variants and seven standalone animation files have been exported. Natural contains 1,757,044 triangles and 13 material slots; Crusher 1,592,454 / 14; IronJaw 1,764,372 / 13; Piston 1,698,988 / 13. Each is one combined skinned mesh and uses the shared material set. The first engine-import candidate preserves high-resolution geometry; any later reductions will follow measured bottlenecks while retaining the cinematic source.

Natural FBX roundtrip passes: 68 bones, 25 morphs, valid UVs/weights, metre-scale bounds, and varying facial controls in every imported clip. Facial controls varying per clip: Idle 9, Walk 7, Run 12, Melee 13, Shoot 9, Hit 13, FacePerformance 17. The report is `Krag_Natural-fbx-roundtrip.json`.

Standalone FBX validation exposed an actual export defect: a rig-only FBX had no Skin/BindPose block and reconstructed a posed reference. Each standalone file now includes a tiny Root-weighted bind carrier mesh, ignored by animation-only engine import, so all 68 reference transforms agree exactly with the assembled Natural (zero measured translation and rotation error). Named takes and varying face controls pass in all seven files; see `standalone-animation-roundtrip.json`. These structural checks are not artistic acceptance.

`../../shared/characters/krag/delivery-v7.json` records the source/runtime hashes and complete FBX/map/manifest inventory. Current shared exports are stable for the first technical engine import. Future source saves use lossless native compression. A content-addressed material reuse path is authored but not executed yet: first seed against the unchanged v7 source/map receipt, then `export_krag.py -- --reuse-tiles` reuses only matching node recipes and file hashes, and rebakes changed materials.


Next source pass (code only): correct the custom mouth cutter orientation and use a narrow bevelled crease, flatten the cheek plane, replace the bib-shaped scarf with a continuous wrapped strip, and add broad canvas wear. No new capture or acceptance is implied by these edits. Shared v7 FBX/map files remain stable for the first native engine comparison.

The verified v7 material-cache seed has completed for all 14 materials; subsequent reuse still requires matching recipe and PNG hashes.
