# Krag source and review record

Status: shared exports are the pretriangulated v7-optimized technical derivative with the root-promoted bounded Shoot aim correction. Latest isolated source is **v9e**, generated and reviewed in four actual views; **artistic and pose acceptance remain false**. This is not an approved merchandise master or a claim of concept-equivalent quality. Engine build/benchmark results are owned by the integration reports.

The visual authority is the original [Krag sheet](../../../krag-kings-design/concept-art/01-krag-character-sheet.png) and [heavy bionics sheet](../../../krag-kings-design/concept-art/07-crusher-claw-and-metal-jaw-sheet.png). The pinned v7 model is original Blender geometry and authored motion, without third-party meshes or motion capture. The isolated v8–v9e source studies adapt provenance-recorded CC0 continuous body and animation-head topology; none has replaced the shared v7 appearance. Skin microstructure incorporates [Mud Cracked Dry 03](https://polyhaven.com/a/mud_cracked_dry_03), a CC0 scan processed by Dario Barresi and photographed by Dimitrios Savva. The downloaded originals, URLs and SHA256 hashes are preserved in `reference-materials/mud_cracked_dry_03/source.json`; diffuse is tinted, relief is restrained and the other material families remain procedural.

`Krag_Master.blend` contains the high-resolution unified anatomy, separate fitted clothing/armor, modular anatomical replacements, 66-bone weighted skeleton in generated v7, 25 facial/body corrective morphs, and seven authored motion actions. The current `../../tools/krag/build_krag.py` and its helpers include **v9e source changes**, so running them does not reproduce the pinned v7 source exactly; use `-- --skip-render` to generate a new review revision without rendering. `AUTHORING_STATUS.json` separates generated evidence from code-only work. The source model faces Blender -Y, with Z up, at metre scale. The proposed regular-Krag crown height is 2.107 m. The ankle rest joints are at X ±0.19 m, Y 0.026 m, Z 0.24 m. These dimensions are benchmark proportions, not newly approved lore.

The natural variant is the first likeness review target. The crusher replaces the full anatomical left arm and adds a mechanical jaw; the separate iron-jaw/eye variant retains natural arms. The right piston-shin variant removes the matching boot and lower-trouser region. Crusher, iron jaw and reinforced piston mechanics are Krag-only; they must not be offered to Nibs.

Animations are authored in place at 30 fps: Idle 61 frames, Walk 37, Run 19, Melee 37, Shoot 31, Hit 28, FacePerformance 180. The rig includes Root, Pelvis, Spine, Chest, Neck, Head, Jaw; paired Clavicle/UpperArm/LowerArm/Hand and Thigh/Shin/Foot/Toe chains; and three claw controls. The full hand silhouette includes four modeled fingers and an opposed thumb, and v5 adds articulated finger/thumb controls. Movement speed, contact correction and action triggering belong to the engine harness.

Review renders are actual meshes. The first and second crusher perspectives exposed excessive eye visibility, separated abdominal volumes, weak finger silhouette, unsuitable footwear geometry and insufficient cloth construction. The third authoring pass addresses those defects and switches the likeness review to the natural variant. The natural front render exposed armor buried in the deltoid and unsuitable spherical steel toe caps; the fourth pass lifts the armor clear, drapes the scarf outside the torso, authors thin formed toe armor and adopts scanned fracture detail. Further comparison is required; these corrections do not constitute approval.

All subsequent heavy authoring, rendering and export tasks run serially through the project's `Run-HeavyTask.ps1` memory guard. Generation, individual view renders and export run as separate processes. The editable master retains source materials; the first explicit PBR bake has completed and its 56 portable maps are present. `../../tools/krag/export_krag.py` prepares portable OpenGL-normal PBR tiles and saves the runtime source (topology reduction is optional and disabled for shape-keyed meshes); a separate fresh invocation with `-- --export-only` exports standalone FBX variants and clips. Each runtime variant combines its selected modular assembly into one skinned mesh with shared material slots, including the right-hand cannon (visible in runtime, omitted from the neutral source likeness review). File existence and importer checks must be verified before claiming delivery.
