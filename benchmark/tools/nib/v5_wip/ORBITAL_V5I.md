The bounded source and three actual diagnostic images exist. Exact hashes and
guard results are in `art/nib/v5-study/native-v5i-orbital-review-result.json`.

uses actual retained orbital topology and the fitted opaque ocular geometry.

`analyze_orbital_rings.py` verifies the earlier actual cache hash, identifies
closed orbital rings, and records their measurements. The source face-set
boundaries are asymmetric; the tight 80-vertex rings lie 12 and 20 graph steps
outside their tagged cavity boundaries. Their position is a fitting proposal,
not a new canonical character measurement.

`audit_saved_orbital_geometry.py` then extracts actual sclera/iris triangles,

preserves the ear-study input and writes an ignored local numerical cache.

`evaluate_orbital_proposal.py --rotation-preservation` reads that verified cache
and the topology report. `orbital_closure_v5i.py` fits neutral clearance and
paired upper/lower arcs against the globe, checks the linear blend's
intermediate clearances, and interpolates into fixed orbital anchors. Its
local-rotation fit preserves the prescribed seam and support. Both nasal
change and nasal expression delta are exactly zero in the numerical study.

diagnostic**: 87 full-Blink fold/canthus triangles turn over 90 degrees from
neutral. There are no new degenerate triangles. Maximum edge stretch is
1.684×. Native generation uses the explicit diagnostic flag and carries this
warning, as well as the inherited oral-visibility failure, into render metadata.
This is not a hidden acceptance bypass or an export-ready asset.
