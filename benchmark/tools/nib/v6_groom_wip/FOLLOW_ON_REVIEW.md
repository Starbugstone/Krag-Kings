# Reference-directed follow-on work

This is a review of original sheet 02 and actual v5d depth/UE baseline images,
not a new design approval. The frozen v5e geometry recipes are unchanged.

## Fur placement and matting

The reference's tawny fur covers the ear exterior and gives the outer silhouette
a soft irregular edge. Cream wisps start at the inner root and rim, sweeping
inward over a still-visible dusty pink membrane. Do not fill the entire inner
ear, thicken cartilage into a solid fur-colored roll, or lay every fiber in a
parallel comb. The crown is layered and tousled around readable goggles; side
tufts follow cheek/skull flow rather than two straight curtains. Sparse fine
fuzz belongs over visible mottled skin, not a full body coat.

The runtime proposal therefore has separate long-wisp and dense short-nap atlas
families, area-weighted/spaced guide roots, region-specific flow and staggered
fiber ends. Short exterior density uses measured eligible area and the actual
nap atlas's alpha coverage; the earlier fixed 160-card count would leave too
much bare surface. A 1.35 projected-coverage target is only a tuning estimate.
Actual overlap, side views, temporal flicker and ear skin visibility still need
rendered proof. Dust may slightly darken roots and gather fibers into irregular
clumps, but must not produce cream paint streaks, uniform black sticks or hard
flat blades. Runtime accents retain 35–80 μm strand radii; card widths represent
multi-fiber clumps, not thicker individual hairs.

The actual UE playful capture at
`benchmark/local/evidence/unreal-showcase/20261006-sandv2-balanced/capture-wgc/unreal-showcase-frames/expression-31.4-nib-playful.png`
still uses the repaired v4b baseline. Its canonical dark-blue tongue is visible,
but protruding eye capsules, nearly absent brows and sparse dark bristles fail
the reference. The new geometry must pass Wary/Tongue/Blink and body-action
closeups as well as neutral views. Fur cannot cover ocular or muzzle failures.

## Garment construction after face/groom

Only after garment shape passes, apply the retained CC0 Brown Leather (.4 m tile)
and Denim Fabric 06 (.3 m tile) detail from `benchmark/art/reference-materials/`.
Keep their original sources/provenance intact. Use their grain/weave/roughness
at credible physical scale with the existing dusty brown palette; denim detail
does not authorize blue jeans. Add localized dust at hems/knees/boot contact,
wear at edges/straps and restrained stitch detail. Uniform noise cannot replace
folds, seams, pocket thickness or an actual normal/roughness bake.

## Face/material work remains separate

v5e is a bounded muzzle correction and retains the clearer eyes/closed neck. It
does not establish the finished sly adult brow/cheek planes, warm irregular
skin mottling, nose pigment or optical eye depth. After its actual depth and
extreme-expression reviews, retain those as explicit sculpt/material tasks.
The nose should be a compact forward brown pad over rounded continuous muzzle
pads/crease. The current goggles are oversized dark discs; compare their fit
and optical readability without hiding the brow in more hair. Do not enlarge
the head or alter canon to accommodate them.
