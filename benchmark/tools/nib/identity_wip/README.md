Current native execution and image judgments are recorded separately in `art/nib/identity-study/orbital-native-v1/actual-result.json` after all four views finish. ## Prepared card-root follow-on

`build_rooted_cards.py` and `fit_card_roots.py` are the retained **failed first native recipe**. Generation stopped before saving at a support-normal gate; the actual read-only attribution and logs are in `art/nib/groom-study/root-attachment-v1`. Thirty-one left outer-ear nap roots and one right rim root compare an authored interpolated vertex normal with a flat side-wall triangle normal. Their nearest surface distances are small; the incompatible normal definitions, rather than established wrong-side roots, caused the rejection. The short audit's initial import-path error is also retained.

The separate `build_rooted_cards_v2.py` and `fit_card_roots_v2.py` are **ungenerated**. The latter reconstructs the same interpolated support normals as the original guide sampler while keeping an independent positive geometric-normal side test and reporting actual triangle-plane burial. It does not lower the original 0.4 like-for-like normal agreement threshold. Its three guarded v2 jobs pin actual orbital source `09da9c1e…` and retain the existing atlas, UVs, topology, card counts and tip vertices. They first require exact point correspondence to the saved guide recipe, then project the first card row onto the actual head/ear skin with a 0.5 mm embedded root and ramp layer crossing from zero at the base. Correction fades to zero by 40% of the guide; only the first two rows can move. Actual inner velvet participates in the ear support surface. Lens-bound exceptions are recorded and preserved rather than silently forced onto the flat goggle envelope. Every unrelated mesh and all bind/actions must survive save/reopen exactly.

Neutral and EyeCloseup use the same source cameras/materials as the actual orbital study. The purpose is to judge the exposed rectangular-root defect separately from a later texture/guide-flow change. Parent's generated atlas exploration failed its production grid and is only an unaccepted visual reference; it is not used here. No successful saved root-attachment source, visual improvement or material/engine acceptance is claimed yet.

## Prepared adult face and ocular follow-on

`adult_face_field.py` and `ocular_identity.py` are separate **ungenerated** work. The former proposes broad supraorbital/cheek relief and a compact projecting nasal/muzzle volume while fixing all actual ocular and oral-domain points. It must derive from the reviewed rooted-card source, preserve the repaired Jaw/lid contact and all bind/actions, and carry attached facial groom with any skin movement. It does not establish new canonical proportions.

The first offline field failed near the oral corners: a 0.4 mm neighboring displacement met fixed edges approximately 0.18 mm long. Its exact recipe is preserved as `adult_face_field_v1_failed.py`. The next numerical construction uses a measured 6 mm geodesic fade into those boundaries before screened graph interpolation. Its archived result has zero introduced neutral degenerates or inverted triangles, maximum displacement 4.23 mm and exact fixed ocular/oral coordinates. These are numerical gates, not actual posed or visual acceptance. Both results remain in `art/nib/identity-study/adult-face-numerical-v1` and `adult-face-numerical-v2`.

The current eye's dark globe inherits the old `Nib_Dark` leather albedo network, while its iris inherits generic `Nib_Eye` skin mottling. Their generic bump was already removed. The new prepared materials `Nib_IdentityOcularGlobe` and `Nib_IdentityOcularIris` preserve the curved opaque shell and real pupil opening, replace those generic fields, and introduce `Nib_IrisCoord` POINT-vector pigment coordinates. The iris **requires an actual assigned-surface UV atlas bake**, never the old yellow reference map or generic UV carrier. Names and requirements were handed to the engine agent. No new transparent cornea, bind/optical geometry change, bake or engine acceptance is claimed.
