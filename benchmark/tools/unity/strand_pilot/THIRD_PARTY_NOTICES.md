# Unity-only shader derivations

The prepared/diagnostic Shader Graphs and local HairVertex subgraph derivative use the official Unity Demo Team Hair and HDRP source nodes identified by the preparation receipts. Their use here is limited to the Unity pilot; the independent Blender curve data is shared with Unreal. Preserve these upstream notices with derived shader files, including those archived under `benchmark/evidence/unity/`.
