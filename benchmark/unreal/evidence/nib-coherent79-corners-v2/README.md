completion markers. Three full variants preserve the 79-bone hierarchy, 25
nonzero morphs, valid skin weights and all seven embedded takes. The seven
standalone takes also pass matching bind and 17-source-pose checks. These are
Blender/FBX results; new engine imports and artistic acceptance are pending.

The raw comparison preserves indexed Basis vertices, original corner UVs and
materials, oriented polygon boundaries and exact split-normal payloads (error 0).

gate. Morph byte identity is explicitly false. The previous split-normal export
rejection is preserved in the adjacent failure evidence.

Natural has 750,292 triangles, Grip 702,894 and Leg 745,184. The delivery contains
27 materials and 102 texture maps, including the unchanged original masked-groom
RGBA maps. This is inventory, not a frame-rate or likeness claim.
