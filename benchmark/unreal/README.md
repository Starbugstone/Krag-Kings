The project uses the shared dune FBX and the same character FBX/PBR exports as Unity. Controls match the [common benchmark contract](../README.md). Character movement uses Unreal CharacterMovement against the imported dune collider; click targets are bounded to the terrain. Both species start in their Natural variants; V cycles to the crusher claw and other bionics. Native animation graph source includes idle/walk/run and action crossfades plus terrain foot IK. Shift+RMB selects walking; ordinary RMB runs. Foot contact hooks and presentation use authored normalized contact phases, terrain ray hits and species-specific strength. Shared CC0 sand-footstep candidates and lit translucent dust are attached; their appearance, synchronization and listening review remain pending. Stance foot IK uses authored stance fractions and world-space planted contacts. E requests the authored FacePerformance clip on the FaceRoot subtree, preserving body motion; C switches to a selected-unit portrait view. Morph targets and custom animation attributes are imported. Blender corrective drivers are reproduced from the explicit bind-relative deformation contract; declared imported morphs and control bones are checked before asset creation. Their actual rendered deformation is still unverified. The selected species' variants cycle independently. Nibs have authored functional replacements only.

Initial renderer settings request DX12/SM6, software Lumen, virtual shadow maps, TSR at native 1080p and Epic scalability. Hardware ray tracing is disabled for the RTX 2060 reference laptop. These are requested settings, not proof of render quality or frame rate; engine compilation, material fidelity, capsule grounding, animation transitions and performance all require real runtime verification. The camera and sunlight exposure also require calibration in the actual engine. Initial framing and fixed exposure now match the Unity baseline (8.5m camera distance, 22° pitch, 38° vertical field of view, 55,000lux sun, EV≈12.7).
